Privacy policy

Abul Hassan Diary ("the app") is a personal diary app for Android. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

What the app stores

Everything you write (days, items, marks, quick words, settings) is stored on your phone only. The app has no account system and no server of its own. The developer cannot see your diary.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect a Google account, the app asks for the Google Drive permission drive.file. This lets the app see and change only the files it creates itself. It cannot see your other Drive files.

  • The app creates one folder in your own Google Drive and saves backup files of your diary there.
  • These files are stored in your Drive, using your Drive storage. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Backups run only after you connect, and only over the internet connection you already use.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the app, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the backup folder from your Drive at any time.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No sharing or selling of data to anyone.
  • The app uses the Google user data only to save and restore your own backups.

Notifications and internet

The app may show a notification if a backup fails. It uses the internet for the Drive backup and, from version 1.0.2, to check whether a newer version of the app is out. For that check it asks GitHub for the number of the latest release. Nothing from your diary or your Google account is sent. As with any website, GitHub receives your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement. The app never downloads or installs an update by itself.
